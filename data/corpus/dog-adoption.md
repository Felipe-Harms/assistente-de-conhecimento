# Dog Adoption Guide

Adopting a dog is a rewarding experience that requires careful preparation.
This guide covers the essentials every new dog owner should know.

## Choosing a Dog

When choosing a dog, consider your living space, energy level, and
lifestyle. Active families may prefer a Border Collie or Labrador, while
apartment dwellers often do well with a French Bulldog or Cavalier King
Charles Spaniel. Always meet the dog in person before committing.

## Vaccination and Health

Every adopted dog should receive a veterinary check-up within the first
week. Core vaccinations include rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and
adenovirus. Heartworm prevention is essential in warmer climates.

## Basic Training

Start with foundational obedience: sit, stay, come, and loose-leash
walking. Positive reinforcement with treats and praise works better than
punishment. Puppy kindergarten classes are an excellent investment.

## Nutrition

Feed a high-quality commercial dog food appropriate for the dog's age,
size, and activity level. Puppies need three meals a day; adult dogs
typically eat twice a day. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, and xylitol.

## Exercise

Dogs need daily exercise. Brisk walks, fetch, and tug-of-war are great
options. The amount varies by breed: working dogs need 1-2 hours, while
smaller companion breeds may be satisfied with 30 minutes.
