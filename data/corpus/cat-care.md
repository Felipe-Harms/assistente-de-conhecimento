# Cat Care Essentials

Cats are independent but require consistent care. This guide covers
feeding, litter, health, and enrichment.

## Feeding Your Cat

Cats are obligate carnivores and need a protein-rich diet. Wet food
helps with hydration, while dry food supports dental health. Feed kittens
three meals a day and adult cats twice a day. Avoid feeding dogs' food
to cats — it lacks taurine.

## Litter Box Setup

Provide one litter box per cat, plus one extra. Place boxes in quiet,
accessible locations. Scoop daily and fully change the litter weekly.
Most cats prefer clumping, unscented litter.

## Health and Vaccinations

Core vaccinations for cats include panleukopenia, herpesvirus, and
calicivirus (the FVRCP combo). Outdoor cats should also receive the
rabies and feline leukemia vaccines. Spay or neuter by five months.

## Enrichment

Cats need mental stimulation. Provide scratching posts, toys, and
window perches. Rotate toys weekly to prevent boredom. Interactive play
with wand toys mimics hunting and reduces behavioral problems.

## Common Health Issues

Watch for signs of urinary problems, dental disease, and obesity.
Annual vet visits help catch issues early. Senior cats (over 10) should
see the vet twice a year.
